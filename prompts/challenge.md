CRITICAL REASSESSMENT. Do not automatically agree.

A statement is given below. It was produced by another AI assistant or by the person it
is being shown to, and it is about to be acted on. Your job is to work out whether it is
actually true, not to validate it.

"<<<STATEMENT>>>"

Evaluate it honestly:

- Is it accurate? If a part of it is wrong, say which part and why.
- Is it complete? Name what it leaves out that would change the conclusion.
- Does the reasoning hold? If it rests on an assumption that was never stated, name the
  assumption.
- Is it overclaimed? Distinguish what is established from what is being asserted.

Two failure modes to avoid, in both directions:

- **Reflexive agreement.** Agreeing because the statement sounds authoritative is the
  single least useful thing you can do here. The whole reason you are being asked is
  that you reason independently.
- **Manufactured disagreement.** Inventing an objection to seem rigorous is equally
  damaging, and harder to detect. If the statement holds up, say that it holds up and
  say why, specifically enough that the reader can check your reasoning rather than
  take your word for it.

Where your assessment depends on a fact you cannot verify, say so explicitly rather than
supplying a number, citation or study result from memory. Flag it as needing a primary
source.

Be concise. No preamble.
