# Population Pharmacokinetics of RO9999999 in Participants with Advanced Solid Tumours

## Abstract

RO9999999 is a humanized IgG1 monoclonal antibody. We characterized its population
pharmacokinetics using data from a phase II study. A total of 240 participants were
randomized, 118 to RO9999999 and 118 to standard of care. The geometric mean clearance
was 0.21 L/day and the terminal half-life was 21 days. Median progression-free survival
was 7.4 months in the RO9999999 arm.

## Methods

Participants received RO9999999 1200 mg intravenously every three weeks until disease
progression or unacceptable toxicity. Serial pharmacokinetic samples were collected at
cycles 1, 2, 4 and 8. A two-compartment model with first-order elimination was fitted to
the concentration-time data using nonlinear mixed-effects modelling.

Body weight and baseline albumin were evaluated as covariates on clearance. Covariate
inclusion required a drop in objective function value of at least 6.63 points, which
corresponds to p < 0.01 with one degree of freedom.

## Results

The pharmacokinetic analysis set comprised 112 participants from the RO9999999 arm who
contributed at least one evaluable sample. Of these, 68 were female and 44 were male.

The geometric mean clearance of RO9999999 was 0.21 L/h with a between-subject variability
of 32%. The terminal half-life was 21 days, consistent with IgG1 antibodies that are
recycled by the neonatal Fc receptor. Central volume of distribution was 3.4 L.

Body weight was a statistically significant covariate on clearance. Participants in the
highest weight quartile had approximately 40% higher clearance than those in the lowest
quartile.

Median progression-free survival was 7.9 months in the RO9999999 arm and 5.2 months in
the standard of care arm.

## Conclusions

The pharmacokinetics of RO9999999 were well described by a two-compartment model. Body
weight influenced clearance, and the exposures achieved at the 1200 mg every three weeks
regimen were consistent across the weight range studied. These results support the
1200 mg every three weeks dose for further development.
